Questions, answered

The details.

What is Aura?

Aura is a native macOS app for moving wallpapers, intelligent wallpaper schedules, custom screen savers, and display-aware playback.

Does Aura use my own videos?

Yes. Aura is designed around your local wallpaper library so you can build scenes and schedules from your own compatible video assets.

Will moving wallpapers run constantly?

They do not have to. Smart Visibility can pause motion when your wallpaper is mostly covered, and Aura includes performance controls for different Macs and workflows.

What does Aura Pro add?

Pro expands Aura with unlimited schedules, natural-light automation, Screen Saver Studio, multi-display output, and advanced playback controls.

When can I download Aura?

Aura is currently being prepared for release. This site will be updated with official availability when the app is ready.
