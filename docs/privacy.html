Privacy Policy

Built to stay on your Mac.

Aura is designed around local files and local processing. This page explains what Aura reads, stores, and shares.

Effective October 4, 2026

Local video and image files

Videos added through Finder remain in their original location. Aura can store read-only security-scoped bookmarks so the sandboxed app can reopen files you selected after relaunch. Images imported into Aura Studio may be copied into Aura's private Application Support storage so projects continue to work. Aura does not upload your wallpaper videos or Aura Studio images as part of its core wallpaper features.

Smart Visibility

To decide whether a moving wallpaper should pause, Aura reads the on-screen geometry of normal application windows through Core Graphics. Aura uses window rectangles, layer numbers, and opacity information for this calculation.

Aura does not capture window pixels, take screenshots, read text from other apps, record your screen, or upload window information as part of Smart Visibility.

Apple Wallpapers

The Apple Wallpapers browser reads compatible wallpaper videos and metadata already downloaded by macOS. Aura does not edit, replace, or delete Apple's wallpaper assets. High-resolution poster images may be generated locally and stored in Aura's cache.

Screen Saver Match

When Screen Saver Match is enabled, Aura may create a local working copy of the current scene in Aura's Application Support directory so the screen saver component can access it. Disabling the feature removes the synced working copy.

Purchases and licensing

When web purchasing is enabled, checkout and payment information is processed by Paddle, Aura's Merchant of Record. Paddle may process information such as your name, email address, billing details, country, payment method information, tax information, and transaction data under Paddle's own privacy terms. Aura does not receive your full payment-card number from Paddle.

Aura may receive order, product, entitlement, and licensing information needed to confirm Aura Pro access, prevent fraud, provide support, and keep purchase status in sync.

Analytics and advertising

Aura's current core app implementation does not include third-party advertising SDKs. If analytics are added in a future release, this policy will be updated before or alongside that change.

Data retention and reset

Local settings and file references remain on your Mac until you remove them, reset Aura, or uninstall the relevant application data. Transaction records held by Paddle are retained according to Paddle's legal and operational requirements.

Your choices

You control which local media you add to Aura and can remove items from Aura without deleting the original files. Mandatory privacy rights available in your jurisdiction are not limited by this policy.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Aura evolves. The current version is the version published on this page.
