Privacy Policy

Built to stay on your Mac.

Aura is designed around local files and local processing. This page explains what Aura reads, stores, and shares.

Effective October 9, 2026

Local video and image files

Videos added through Finder remain in their original location. Aura can store read-only security-scoped bookmarks so the sandboxed app can reopen files you selected after relaunch. Images imported into Aura Studio may be copied into Aura's private Application Support storage so projects continue to work. Aura does not upload your wallpaper videos or Aura Studio images as part of its core wallpaper features.

Smart Visibility

To decide whether a moving wallpaper should pause, Aura reads the on-screen geometry of normal application windows through Core Graphics. Aura uses window rectangles, layer numbers, and opacity information for this calculation.

Aura does not capture window pixels, take screenshots, read text from other apps, record your screen, or upload window information as part of Smart Visibility.

Apple Wallpapers

Aura reads compatible wallpaper videos and metadata already downloaded by macOS. It does not alter or delete existing Apple wallpaper files. If you explicitly grant access to the macOS aerials cache, Aura can add newly downloaded compatible wallpaper videos to that folder. It otherwise saves downloads in its own Library. Poster images may be generated locally and stored in Aura's cache.

Screen Saver Match

With your permission, Aura may copy the selected scene into an Aura-managed working folder so its separately installed macOS Screen Saver companion can display it. Aura retains your original media and independent editable designs. Applied working files can persist until replaced or removed.

Weather and precise location

When you enable weather-dependent features, Aura may use high-accuracy Mac location coordinates or coordinates you choose to request weather forecasts. For supported U.S. locations, Aura contacts the U.S. NOAA National Weather Service (NWS); elsewhere or if NWS is unavailable, it contacts the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway). These providers receive the requested coordinates and standard connection information such as your IP address, which may be retained in their logs. Weather information is cached locally and reused by the Screen Saver. MET Norway forecast data is available under CC BY 4.0 with source attribution in Aura. Aura does not use weather information for advertising.

Optional Sign in with Apple and iCloud

Sign in with Apple is optional. Aura stores your Apple-provided user identifier and any name or email shared with Aura locally on your Mac. Delete Aura Account removes this local record and explains how to revoke Apple's separate authorization in Apple Account settings. Optional iCloud Backup saves supported preferences and small designs in your private CloudKit database. You can disable future backups and delete your private cloud backup separately. This does not cancel App Store subscriptions.

Purchases and licensing

The Mac App Store version of Aura uses Apple's StoreKit to offer monthly and lifetime Aura Pro purchases. Purchases, renewals, refunds and subscription management for this version are handled by Apple, not through an in-app website checkout. Aura verifies Apple purchase entitlements to unlock features. Aura does not receive your full Apple payment-card number.

If separate direct purchases are offered on Aura's website, those transactions are handled by Paddle as a separate merchant of record. Paddle may process billing, identity, payment and tax information under its own privacy terms for purchases made through that channel; this does not mean the Mac App Store app has an external checkout.

Analytics and advertising

Aura's current core app implementation does not include third-party advertising SDKs. If analytics are added in a future release, this policy will be updated before or alongside that change.

Data retention and reset

Local settings and file references remain on your Mac until you remove them, reset Aura or uninstall its data. Your original media is not deleted by account deletion. Apple retains Mac App Store transaction information under its own policies. If separate web purchases occur, Paddle retains their transactions according to its own policies. Third-party weather providers may retain requested coordinates in server access logs.

Your choices

You control which local media you add to Aura and can remove items from Aura without deleting the original files. Mandatory privacy rights available in your jurisdiction are not limited by this policy.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Aura evolves. The current version is the version published on this page.
