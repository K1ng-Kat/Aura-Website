Refund Policy

Help with App Store purchases.

Aura Pro purchases and refund decisions are managed by Apple.

Updated October 2026

App Store purchases and refunds

Aura Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases made through Apple's Mac App Store are billed by Apple. To request a refund, visit Apple's Report a Problem website and follow Apple's instructions. Refund eligibility and processing are determined by Apple under its applicable policies and local law.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Monthly and other applicable auto-renewing Aura Pro subscriptions are managed through your Apple Account subscription settings. Canceling stops future renewals subject to Apple's terms; deleting your Aura account does not cancel App Store billing.

One-time Lifetime access

Lifetime access is a one-time in-app purchase rather than an automatically renewing subscription. Apple handles any refund request for that purchase.

Technical support

If something is not working in Aura, visit our Support page. Please do not send payment card details when requesting help.

Mandatory consumer rights

Nothing on this page limits any cancellation, refund, warranty, or other rights that apply under law.
