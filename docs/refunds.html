Refund Policy

A clear path if a purchase does not work out.

This policy applies to Aura Pro purchases completed through Paddle.

Effective October 4, 2026

14-day request window

You may request a refund within 14 days of an initial Aura Pro purchase or a subscription renewal. Requests are reviewed fairly, taking into account the reason for the request, product usage, applicable law, and Paddle's buyer terms.

Common reasons we will consider

  • An accidental or duplicate purchase.
  • A technical problem that prevents you from using a core paid feature and cannot be resolved through reasonable troubleshooting.
  • A purchase made under a mistaken understanding of what Aura Pro includes, when reported promptly.
  • Any situation where applicable law requires a refund, cancellation right, or other remedy.

Monthly subscriptions

Canceling a monthly subscription stops future renewals but does not automatically refund an already-paid period. If you want a refund of a recent renewal, submit a refund request within the window above.

Lifetime purchases

The lifetime option is a one-time purchase for ongoing Aura Pro access to the supported Aura product. Refund requests for lifetime purchases follow the same policy and 14-day request window.

How refunds are issued

Paddle is Aura's Merchant of Record and seller for web transactions. Approved refunds are issued by Paddle back to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment method and financial institution.

How to request a refund

Use the buyer-support or order-management link provided in your Paddle receipt or purchase confirmation. Include the email address used for the order and a short explanation of the request. Do not send payment-card details to Aura.

Mandatory rights

Nothing in this policy limits refund, withdrawal, cancellation, warranty, or consumer rights that apply to you under law.
